# Mohamed Aniss Benstaali

I publish under the handle **Uchiha Mabben**. This site is my research notebook for cybersecurity, privacy engineering, and the craft of turning technical questions into reproducible evidence.

My work currently sits at the intersection of:

- application and systems security;
- privacy-preserving data analysis;
- defensive engineering and detection;
- reproducible security experimentation.

I completed my final-year research project at the École Nationale Supérieure d'Informatique (ESI), studying a variance-adaptive refinement of Private Multiplicative Weights for repeated queries over sensitive data.

## Research principles

**Evidence before confidence.** Claims should be backed by observable behavior, artifacts, or a derivation that another researcher can inspect.

**Reproduction before publication.** A result is more useful when its environment, assumptions, and failure cases are documented.

**Defense alongside offense.** Technical analysis should explain impact, detection opportunities, and mitigations—not only how a weakness works.

**Authorization and coordinated disclosure.** All experiments described here are performed in controlled environments or against systems where testing is explicitly authorized.
