I support coordinated vulnerability disclosure and research performed with explicit authorization.

## Reporting an issue

If you discover a security issue in one of my repositories:

1. Use GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting** feature when it is available.
2. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact. Do not include exploit details, credentials, personal data, or other sensitive evidence in a public issue.
3. Include the affected component and version, prerequisites, reproduction steps, observed impact, and any proposed remediation.

## Handling reports

I will validate the report, preserve attribution where requested, and coordinate publication around a fix or an agreed disclosure date. Timelines depend on severity, reproducibility, and downstream impact.

## Research boundaries

Content on this site is intended for education, authorized testing, and defensive improvement. Examples use controlled environments or sanitized artifacts. Publication does not grant permission to test systems you do not own or administer.

> Do not send live credentials, private keys, medical records, or unnecessary personal information with a report.
